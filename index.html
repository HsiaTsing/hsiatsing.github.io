Research

My research integrates medical artificial intelligence, computer vision, and geometric computing to develop reliable models for biomedical discovery, quantitative diagnosis, and intelligent intervention.

Virtual Cell & Multi-omics Modeling

Developing foundation and generative models that unify genomic, transcriptomic, proteomic, metabolomic, and molecular data to represent and simulate cellular systems. We focus on perturbation prediction, mechanistic inference, and AI-driven scientific discovery to accelerate target identification, therapeutic design, and efficacy evaluation.

Multi-omics Foundation Models · Perturbation Prediction · Mechanistic Modeling · AI-driven Drug Discovery

Multimodal Computational Pathology

Developing multimodal computational pathology methods that integrate whole-slide morphology with genomic, transcriptomic, and imaging-derived phenotypes across tissue, regional, and cellular scales. We focus on foundation models for cancer diagnosis, prognosis, and evidence-grounded reporting with spatially localized and interpretable predictions.

Multimodal Pathology · Cellular Foundation Models · Cancer Diagnosis & Prognosis · Evidence-grounded Reporting

Medical Image Analysis & Organ Modeling

Developing robust methods for structured analysis and modeling of 2D and 3D medical images across modalities. We focus on segmentation, detection, cross-modal registration, image generation, and anatomical reconstruction to support quantitative phenotyping, diagnosis, and treatment planning.

Medical Image Segmentation · Cross-modal Registration · Image Generation · Anatomical Modeling

3D Vision & Geometric Modeling

Developing representation and learning methods for reconstructing, analyzing, and editing three-dimensional shapes and dynamic scenes. We focus on geometry-aware interpolation, deformation, motion capture, animation, and generative modeling that preserve structural fidelity, controllability, and temporal coherence.

3D Shape Representation · Geometry Processing · Motion & Deformation · Learning-based Modeling

Surgical Scene Intelligence & Decision-making

Developing integrated intelligence for perceiving, reconstructing, and reasoning about dynamic surgical environments. We combine editable 3D/4D reconstruction with surgical video understanding, workflow modeling, and context-aware planning to enable precise assistance and autonomous decision-making.

Surgical Scene Reconstruction · Surgical Video Understanding · Workflow Intelligence · Autonomous Decision-making
